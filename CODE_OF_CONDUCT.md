# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors and maintainers are expected to be respectful, direct, and constructive. Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies to project discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository's available maintainer or security-reporting channel. Reports will be reviewed as promptly as practical and handled with discretion.

Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to keep the project safe and productive.
